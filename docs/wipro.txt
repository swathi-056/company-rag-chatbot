1. COMPANY OVERVIEW
Wipro Limited is a global AI-powered technology services and consulting company. Wipro provides IT transformation, consulting and business process services and helps clients address digital transformation needs.

2. CURRENT COMPANY SNAPSHOT
According to Wipro's current corporate overview:
- Annual revenue: $10.48 billion for the financial year ended March 2026
- Employees: 240,000+
- Countries: 65
- More than 146 nationalities represented
- 80 years of progress and purpose
- More than 25 years listed on the NYSE

3. HISTORY
Wipro was incorporated on December 29, 1945, originally as Western India Vegetable Products Limited. It began as a vegetable-oil manufacturer in Amalner, Maharashtra, and later expanded into soaps, consumer care and eventually information technology and services.
