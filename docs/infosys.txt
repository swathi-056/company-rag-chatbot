Last verified: August 2026
Purpose: RAG knowledge base for company/career questions.

1. COMPANY OVERVIEW
Infosys Limited is a global consulting and IT services company founded in 1981. Infosys describes itself as a global leader in next-generation digital services and consulting. Its purpose is to amplify human potential and create the next opportunity for people, businesses and communities.

2. CURRENT COMPANY SNAPSHOT
- Founded: 1981
- Global presence: 59 countries
- Employees: 328,000+ according to the current Infosys corporate overview
- Latest company overview reports more than 40 years of service.
- Infosys is listed on the NYSE.
- Infosys has evolved from an Indian software-services company into a global digital transformation, consulting, cloud and AI services organization.
