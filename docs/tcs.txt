COMPANY KNOWLEDGE BASE: TATA CONSULTANCY SERVICES (TCS)
Last verified: August 2026
Purpose: RAG knowledge base for company/career questions.

1. COMPANY OVERVIEW
Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) is a technology services, consulting and business solutions organization and part of the Tata Group. TCS was founded in 1968 and works with organizations worldwide on technology and business transformation.

2. CURRENT COMPANY SNAPSHOT
- Founded: 1968
- Part of: Tata Group
- Global presence: 55+ countries / TCS corporate pages describe a workforce across more than 50 countries; the current careers page reports nearly 600,000 associates across 55 countries as of June 30, 2026.
- Service delivery: The current careers page reports more than 200 service delivery centres.
- TCS has an aspiration to become the world's largest AI-led technology services company.
- TCS emphasizes long-term relationships, innovation, engineering excellence and continuous learning.

8. ELIGIBILITY AND RECRUITMENT
TCS recruitment criteria are not permanently fixed. Eligibility can depend on:
- Graduation year
- Degree/branch
- Academic performance
- Backlogs
- Gap rules
- Experience
- Skills
- Specific hiring program

Selection stages vary by program. Some campus or graduate hiring programs may include an online assessment followed by interviews, but candidates must verify the current notification.

9. TCS NQT / ASSESSMENTS
TCS conducts different hiring and assessment programs over time. Do not assume that every TCS recruitment uses exactly the same test pattern. When preparing for an active hiring drive, use the current official notification and assessment instructions.
